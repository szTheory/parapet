Logo — Round 2

A serious rethink after Round 1: a custom cut-stone wordmark with the rook / parapet crenellation worked into the letters themselves — not an icon beside text. The word and the motif are the same object.

Round 1 (A/B/C/D) is archived in logo-options.html — superseded by this round. Crenellation here is a true transparent cut (works on any background). Wordmarks are hand-built geometry (font-independent), so no IBM Plex install is needed to view them.

T1 — Rampart

integrated typemarkrecommended

Crenellation carved across the whole word — "PARAPET" is a parapet wall. Fully legible, bold, unmistakably on-brand. Watch Blue stays a small accent (in the mark/favicon), keeping the wordmark calm.

T4 — Rampart + sightline

integrated typemark + signal

T1 plus a Watch Blue telemetry sightline threading along the base through arrow-slits in the stems. More overtly SRE/telemetry — richer, but busier and harder at small sizes.

T3 — Rook P lead

hero letter

Mostly plain cut-stone caps; only the initial P is a rook tower (crenellated crown + a Watch Blue arrow-slit). Subtler — reads as a conventional wordmark with one hero letter.

T2 — Two rooks

out

The two A's get crowns — but as bumps-on-top they read as an umlaut ("PÄRÄPET"). The same failure mode as Round 1's crown. Shown for the record; not advancing.
