## TL;DR

This audit was run on an **incomplete milestone**. Two distinct problems:

1. **Phases 27, 28, 29 are not started** — 12 of 24 requirements are unsatisfied because the playbooks (PB-01..06), demo seed + CI lane (DEMO-05/06), and stability/onboarding (STAB-07, ADOP-01..03) phases have no plans and no code.

Phase 25 verified the demo-app copy and marked UI-04 satisfied, but the thing adopters actually generate crashes with a `CaseClauseError` on those branches.

The recovery DB loop itself (Preview → claim-protected Confirm → TimelineEntry + ToolAudit) **is** correctly wired and works.

Verified by reading source + the passing concurrency/unboxed tests.

**Two functional blockers (NOT caught by per-phase verification):**

### INT-01 (CRITICAL) — Generator template crashes on new return variants → breaks UI-04 for adopters

- `priv/templates/parapet.gen.ui/operator_detail_live.ex.eex:133-141` is a 2-arm `case` matching only `{:ok, _result}` and `{:error, reason}`.
- `Parapet.Operator.confirm_runbook_step/4` returns `{:short_circuited, reason}` and `{:conflicted, claim_id}`. Neither matches → **`CaseClauseError` at runtime** in any adopter-generated LiveView.
- The **demo app** copy (`examples/demo_app/.../operator_detail_live.ex:134-164`) HAS all 4 arms + `short_circuit_flash/1`. Phase 25 verified that copy and marked UI-04 satisfied. Plan 25-02 was scoped to "Demo LiveView" only — the generator template (last touched in v0.9, commit b76279e) was never updated.
- **Impact:** UI-04 ("the LiveView renders both branches with operator-actionable next steps") is true for the demo but false for every adopter who runs `mix parapet.gen.ui`. Per the adopter-first "done" lens, this is a shipped-but-broken defect.

### INT-02 (HIGH) — Telemetry contract documented-but-unwired

- `lib/parapet/telemetry/recovery_action.ex` defines an 8-event frozen family. There are 29 `:telemetry.execute/span` call-sites in `lib/`, **zero** of which reference `recovery_action`.
- FND-02 only requires the contract be *documented* (satisfied), and Phase 23 SC-4 explicitly deferred emission ("a future capability addition can wire a new emit-site"). But no remaining v1.1 phase (27/28/29) wires emit-sites, so the contract ships dead.
- **Impact:** adopters who attach `:telemetry` handlers to the documented events get nothing. Either wire emit-sites in the Confirm path during a remaining phase, or explicitly document that the contract ships ahead of its emitters.
