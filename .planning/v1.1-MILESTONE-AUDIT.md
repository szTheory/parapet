# Milestone v1.1 "Actionable Recovery" — Audit Report

**Audited:** 2026-05-29
**Status:** ⚡ tech_debt (no blockers; accumulated deferred items to review)
**Headline:** All 7 phases complete, all 24 requirements satisfied, end-to-end recovery loop wired and proven. No functional blockers. The remaining items are documentation reconciliation, incomplete Nyquist validation artifacts, and minor UX/copy polish.

## TL;DR

The v1.1 action loop is **done and works end-to-end**: an operator clicks Preview → Confirm on a runbook step, the Confirm routes through `ClaimService.claim_action/1` (claim-protected, `action_kind: "operator"`, lease/self-heal), executes the host-registered capability, writes a `TimelineEntry` + `ToolAudit`, and surfaces inline in the retrospective. The demo app seeds a Confirm-able incident and CI exercises four scenarios (happy-path, expired-preview, short-circuit, claim-conflict) plus a headless LiveView click-through. Six runbook templates ship, `Parapet.Recovery` is Stable, and adopters get a generator (`mix parapet.gen.recovery`), a doctor signal, and a guide.

**The previous (2026-05-28) audit reported `gaps_found` — that audit is now superseded.** It was run mid-milestone (phases 27/28/29 unbuilt) and flagged two critical/high integration defects plus an open UI bug. All three are now fixed in the codebase (commit `7737945`). See [Resolved Since Last Audit](#resolved-since-last-audit).
